Actions you cannot undo1.2.21.3.84.3.2

  • irreversible Turning on Key Vault purge protection.
  • irreversible Locking an immutable Recovery Services vault, and always-on soft delete.
  • irreversible Removing Everyone as Security Copilot contributor: it cannot be added back.
  • irreversible Purging anything soft-deleted.
  • Lockout risk: a Conditional Access policy with no break-glass exclusion; removing your standing admin role before proving PIM activation.
